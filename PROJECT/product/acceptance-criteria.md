# Product Acceptance Criteria

A story is accepted only when:
- The authorized role can complete its intended flow.
- Unauthorized and cross-tenant attempts are denied.
- Input is schema-validated and errors are actionable.
- Loading, empty, failure, denied and success states are present.
- Relevant privileged actions emit append-only audit events.
- Tests cover normal, boundary and abuse cases.
- Keyboard accessibility and responsive behavior are verified for UI flows.
- Telemetry is useful without collecting secrets or unnecessary personal data.
- Documentation and rollback implications are updated.

For AI-generated plans, acceptance also includes editable task breakdown, dependency validation, budget constraints and a human approval checkpoint before execution.
