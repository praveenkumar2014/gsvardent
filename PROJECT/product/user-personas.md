# User Personas

## Founder / Executive
Needs outcomes, priorities, spend visibility, risk visibility and reliable delivery evidence. Prefers clear summaries and approval controls.

## Staff Engineer
Needs inspectable plans, typed contracts, diffs, reproducible tests, terminal access and exact control over credentials and environments.

## Product Operator
Needs to convert initiatives into milestones, tasks, owners and acceptance criteria without manually writing engineering tickets.

## Investor
Needs approved read-only portfolio status and metrics, not access to source code, credentials or operational controls.

## End User
Needs a guided workspace, plain-language plan review, clear progress, safe approvals and accessible recovery paths.

Personas are hypotheses until validated through research. Do not infer protected or sensitive characteristics.
