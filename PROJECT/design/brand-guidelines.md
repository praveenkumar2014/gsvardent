# Verdent Brand Guidelines

## Brand attributes
Precise, calm, capable, trustworthy and human-controlled. The product should feel like a professional engineering workspace rather than an uncontrolled chatbot.

## Visual direction
Use a restrained dark-first interface with clear surface hierarchy, readable code/text, disciplined accent color and subtle motion. Light mode must remain equally complete and accessible.

## Logo and naming
Use the Verdent wordmark and approved brand assets only. Do not insert third-party platform branding as the product identity. Maintain correct clear space, contrast and minimum size.

## Content voice
Use direct language, plain descriptions of system state and explicit permission/cost language. Avoid claims such as “fully autonomous,” “secure by default” or “deployed” unless the implementation and evidence justify them.
