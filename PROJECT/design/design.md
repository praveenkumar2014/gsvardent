# UX Design Principles

- Human control over autonomy: preview, approval and undo/rollback where feasible.
- Clear mental model: separate projects, plans, tasks, agents, tools and artifacts.
- Progressive disclosure: surface risk, permissions and costs at decision points.
- Explainability: show why a plan exists, what each agent may access and what changed.
- Consistency: predictable status, navigation, actions and feedback.
- Accessibility: keyboard-first operation, semantic landmarks, visible focus and adequate contrast.
- Trust: never imply an action ran, deployed or succeeded until system evidence confirms it.
- Recovery: make cancellation, retry, failure details and support paths discoverable.
