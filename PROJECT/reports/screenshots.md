# Screenshots and Review Evidence

Store approved UI screenshots, flow recordings and accessibility evidence here or in the approved design/evidence system. Do not commit secrets, personal data, live customer content or environment URLs containing credentials.

For each evidence item record:
- Screen/flow and role
- Viewport and theme
- Commit or prototype version
- Test scenario
- Expected and observed outcome
- Known limitations

No application screenshots are available in the repository baseline yet; do not imply implemented screens exist.
